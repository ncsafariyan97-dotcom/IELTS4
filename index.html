Technology

How technology has changed our lives and the way we communicate, work, study, and entertain ourselves.

IELTS Speaking Focus

• Personal experiences with technology

• Advantages and disadvantages

• Technology in education and work

• AI and future developments

• Social media and digital communication

Band 8+ Tip

Don't just say:

Technology is useful.

Instead:

Technology has revolutionized the way we communicate, access information, and carry out everyday tasks.

Answer Development

Don't Give Short Answers

Weak Answer

Yes, I use technology a lot. It's useful.

Better Answer

Yes, I use technology quite extensively because it makes my daily routine more efficient. For example, I rely on several apps to organize my work and communicate with colleagues. As a result, I can save a considerable amount of time.
WHAT → WHY → EXAMPLE → RESULT

IELTS Tip: Don't stop after giving your opinion. Develop your answer with a reason, an example, and a result.

Common Mistakes

Common IELTS Mistakes with "Technology"

1. Technology vs. Technologies

I love technologies.

✓ I love technology.

✓ I love modern technologies.

Use technology for technology in general. Use technologies for different types or systems.

2. Technology vs. Technique

Learning technology is important for speaking English.

✓ Learning new techniques is important for speaking English.

Technology = tools, machines, systems
Technique = a method or skill

3. Repetition

Technology is important because technology helps people and technology improves life.

✓ Technology is important because it helps people and improves their quality of life.

Try: innovation • digital tools • technological advancement • modern devices

Common Mistakes

More Mistakes to Avoid

4. Incorrect Preposition

Depend to technology

Depend of technology

✓ Depend on technology

5. Wrong Adjective Form

Technology development

✓ Technological development

✓ Technological advancement

6. Only "Good" or "Bad"

Technology is good.

✓ Technology is highly beneficial.

✓ Technology is a double-edged sword.

✓ Technology has transformed the way we live and work.

Error Correction

Fix the IELTS Answer

Find the mistakes:

Technology is very good because technology helps people and technology is important. People depend to technology and there are many technology development nowadays.
Improved version:

There's no denying that technology plays a crucial role in modern life. It helps people save time and improve their productivity. However, many people have become overly dependent on technology, and technological development is happening at an unprecedented pace.

IELTS Tips

Technology: Band 7–9 Tips

1. Don't memorize full answers.
Memorize useful language, not complete responses.
2. Develop your ideas.
Don't stop after giving your opinion.
3. Use vocabulary naturally.
One well-used advanced word is better than five forced words.
4. Show grammatical range.
Mix simple and complex structures.
5. Give examples.
Examples make your ideas clearer and more convincing.
6. Avoid repetition.
Use pronouns, synonyms, and paraphrasing.
7. Don't force idioms.
Use them only when they fit naturally.
8. Answer directly.
Especially in Part 3, make your position clear.
